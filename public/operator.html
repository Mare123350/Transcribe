Privacy and saving

Audio is never saved by this app. Transcripts stay in memory and disappear when this page closes.

Where the audio goes while listening

  • Deepgram: audio passes through this server to Deepgram for transcription. The app asks Deepgram not to keep it for model improvement.
  • Browser speech recognition: the browser handles the audio. Chrome sends it to Google to transcribe.
